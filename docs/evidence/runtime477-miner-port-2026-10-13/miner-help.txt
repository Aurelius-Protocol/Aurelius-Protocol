usage: aurelius-miner [-h]
                      {validate,balance,economics,ready,observe,pool,status,dashboard,deposit,fund,serve}
                      ...

positional arguments:
  {validate,balance,economics,ready,observe,pool,status,dashboard,deposit,fund,serve}
    validate            validate staged items against the published
                        config_schema
    balance             report affordability + the deposit address
    economics           read own balance, signed prices and recent receipts
    ready               check local candidate eligibility, not
                        funding/discovery
    observe             refresh charge/refund and validator-progress
                        observations for recent attempts
    pool                list or enable/disable staged items, preserving
                        history
    status              read the local attempt journal
    dashboard           read-only localhost observation dashboard
    deposit             resolve the deposit address (unlocks the coldkey)
    fund                preview or submit one native-alpha WT purchase on
                        subnet 37
    serve               serve /v1/query (mint a fresh work_id per request)

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
